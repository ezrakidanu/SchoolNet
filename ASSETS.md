# SchoolNet assets

The SchoolNet logo (`static/images/logo.svg`) is an original vector mark made for this MVP. Staff avatars are intentional colored monograms for the fictional Mr. Abebe, Ms. Sara, and Coach Dan profiles; they do not depict real people.

The illustrative Science Club photo (`static/images/science-lab.jpg`) was retrieved from the [Unsplash laboratory-science collection](https://unsplash.com/s/photos/laboratory-science) through image search. It depicts a microscope and lab equipment. Before commercial redistribution, verify the original photograph's applicable license and attribution requirements or replace it with your own school-approved image.

Typography uses DM Sans and Manrope through Google Fonts when an internet connection is available. The stylesheet includes system-font fallbacks, and all app code, logo assets, and the feed image are served locally. No external JavaScript frameworks, tracking SDKs, or icon libraries are required by the application.
